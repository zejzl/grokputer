# Wings Against the Morning Sky

Aloft on currents high above the trees,
They trace their paths through clouds with fleeting grace,
Each feathered form that dances on the breeze
Knows freedom that no earthbound thing can chase.

From dawn's first light to dusk's descending veil,
They sing their songs of territory, love,
With notes that rise and fall, ascend and sail—
A symphony conducted from above.

The hawk glides silent, scanning earth below,
While sparrows flit in restless, chattering bands,
The heron stands in shallows, still and slow,
Each species writes its story on the lands.

In flight they teach us: rise and never fear,
For home is not a place, but atmosphere.
