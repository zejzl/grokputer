### thoth.py: Emerald Tablets of Thoth
This file summarizes the 12 Emerald Tablets, attributed to Thoth, the Atlantean priest-king who fled to Egypt after Atlantis's destruction around 52,000 BC, ruling for 16,000 years and building the Great Pyramid of Giza as a repository for Atlantean knowledge. Thoth, later deified as the God of Wisdom and reincarnated as Hermes, engraved the tablets in Atlantean language, recovered in 1925. The tablets cover his history, the Halls of Amenti (eternal fire-bathed chambers for the living and dead), keys to wisdom (balancing material/immaterial realms via inner fire and meditation), space-born origins of humanity (souls as star-children bound to bodies), the Dweller of Unal (a cosmic master who sank Atlantis for forbidden knowledge), keys of magic (battling darkness with light and vibrations), the Seven Lords (guides from beyond space-time), and mysteries of formlessness. Overall themes revolve around immortality through knowledge, cosmic laws, reincarnation, vibration-based enlightenment, and the pursuit of light over darkness, blending Atlantis mythology with esoteric teachings on self-transformation and oneness with the universe.
